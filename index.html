Medical Practice

Context

For my Freedom Project I picked the topic Medical Practice because I want to work in the medical field when I'm older. I picked medical practice. I searched for medical practice apps and found three: Virtual Practice Healthcare, Jotform Health, and the NHS App. Virtual Practice Healthcare looked the most interesting to me, especially because its logo is a stethoscope. Healthcare technology is growing fast, mostly through mobile apps. Apps like Virtual Practice Healthcare let doctors and patients talk online, so people can get care from home. Instead of going to a doctor's office, patients can have video calls, ask questions, and even get prescriptions right from their phone. This makes healthcare easier and more handy, especially for people who can't get to a clinic or just don't want to leave home.

Part A: Existing Technology

Hardware

Glucose Meter

A glucose meter is a small device that runs on batteries to measure sugar levels in your blood. You place a drop of blood on a test strip and insert it into the meter. You should test before eating breakfast, since food can change the results. Both high and low blood sugar can be dangerous.

Defibrillator

A defibrillator is a machine that sends an electric shock to the heart to help it beat normally again during a heart attack or abnormal rhythm. It has big batteries, wires, high-voltage parts, paddles or sticky pads, buttons, a screen, and an ECG monitor to check the heartbeat. Some defibrillators are automated (AED) and can work by themselves. It is used in emergencies to restart the heart and save lives.

Ventilator

A ventilator helps or takes over a patient's breathing. It delivers air or oxygen through a gas flow system, uses sensors to check pressure and volume, and has a user interface that lets doctors adjust settings. Alarms warn of problems, and circuits connect the machine to the patient. It is used in the ICU, during surgery, for respiratory failure, or under anesthesia.

Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) Scanner

An MRI machine uses strong magnets and radio waves to make detailed pictures of the inside of the body. The magnet creates a strong magnetic field, gradient coils adjust the field to focus on specific areas, and RF coils send and receive radio signals. A computer processes the data and the display shows the images. It is used for detailed imaging of the brain, spine, joints, and soft tissues.

Software

Epic Systems

Epic is a huge software used by hospitals and big medical offices to keep track of patient info, like health records and appointments. It keeps track of medical history, lets doctors do video visits, schedules appointments, and handles billing. This helps doctors find your info faster, make fewer mistakes, and keep your data safe.

Zocdoc

Zocdoc is an app/website where you can book doctor appointments online. You can find doctors, check their ratings, and book a visit without having to call. You can search for doctors by what you need, like a dentist, by location, and by which insurance they accept. You can read patient reviews, book same-day appointments, and even do video visits. It helps by saving you time, letting you find last-minute openings, and helping you choose a good doctor based on what others say.

Part B: Future Technology

For my future technology I came up with a lot of technologies that can be used to improve peoples lives and make it easier. Some of the technologies are Robotic surgery, 3D printing, Finger Scanning, Mind Machine, and a teleportation device. Robotic surgery allows doctors to perform complicated procedures with steady hands and speed. It leads to smaller cuts, quicker recovery, and fewer problems for patients. 3D printing creates custom implants, prosthetics, and even organs that fit exactly to a patient's body. This is especially helpful when a donor cannot be found in time. With finger scanning, your finger holds all your medical information. When you scan your finger at the doctor's office, your info appears on the screen, so you don't need to carry insurance cards. It also increases security and reduces mistakes in managing patient information. A mind machine connects directly to the brain. It can help doctors know exactly where you feel pain without you having to fill out paperwork. It can also read your thoughts to detect early signs of mental health conditions.Teleportation could help when you need to go to the hospital urgently but an ambulance cannot get through. It could also allow someone to travel across the world to get an organ and bring it back quickly and without damage. Although it sounds unrealistic, it is a real need in the medical field.
