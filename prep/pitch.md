### Pitch
Most people think that seeing a doctor means sitting in a waiting room for hours. But what if you could talk to a doctor, get medicine, or even recieve a new organ without leaving your home?
My website about Medical Practice shows how it could happen. First I found tools that we already use like the NHS App, Epic Systems, and Zocdoc. These tools allow people to book appointments, see their health records, and talk to doctors online. 
Then I thought of future technologies that could improve peoples lives. The main technology I thought about was a teleportation device. 
Imagine a person having a heart attack gets sent to a specialist in seconds. Or a donor organ arrives right away without needing a plane. It sounds like a movie, but when every second counts teleportation could save lives.
