#### Takeaways from in-class presentation
Some takeaways I have from the in class presentation is to not be afraid to speak infront of everyone, because while I was presenting everyon was just looking at me and it felt overwhelming. But other than that I think another takeaway is to be more prepared because I forgot some parts of what I wanted to say during my presentation. Especially things that could interest the audience. 

#### Takeaways from Expo elevator pitch
One takeaway I have from giving my elevator pitch is to go with the flow. I had a index card with what I was going to say, and I used it to memorize my pitch, but I didn't want to be reading word for word. So I memoriezed the hook and if I didn't know what else to say I would quickly glance at it. At first I practiced giving my pitch to some of my friends and it helped me be more confident on what to say. When I was in the expo I saw the judges coming to my table and I felt nervous and scared that I was going to stutter even after all the practicing. I first showed my exisiting technologies I researched and I talked about what all of them had in common, and I flowed into my future technology I came up with. I showed the AI generated picture of the teleportation device first, then I showed my A-frame, and explained why I made AI generated model to better visualize the future technology. I finished the pitch with a thank you. Every time I presented I found myself going with what feels right and not word for word of what I had, I was adding ideas as I was currently presenting to the judges or students. 

### Engineering Design Process
After going beyond our MVP for our freedom project we finally started preping for presentations. We made google slides to have our in-class presentations, I really wanted it to look nice so I went on Canva and found good templates to use for my background. I found 5 good templates that stood out to me and it was difficult for me to choose one. So I started adding some text to see how big and small I would have to make the size, or the color scheme, and especailly the layout of how I wanted to put things. On some templates the background was really distracting and the text was harder to read so I removed it from my options. Then I eventually decided on the one I have now, although its dark and might be slightly hard to read dark text on it, its better than the ones from before. Then after our in-class presentations we started doing our elevator pitch for the expo because we only had ONE minute to present to the judges because they had to see everyone and they can't spend 10 minutes on my project. An elevtor pitch is a informal presentation while the slides was formal. I found it difficult to make all the information on my slides be a 60 second pitch, how am I supposed to say everything I need to in a short amount of time. 


### Skills
