#### Takeaways from Expo elevator pitch
One take away I have from giving my elevator pitch is to go with the flow. I had a index card with what I was going to say, and I used it to memorize my pitch, but I didn't want to be reading word for word. So I memoriezed the hook and if I didn't know what else to say I would quickly glance at it. At first I practiced giving my pitch to some of my friends and it helped me be more confident on what to say. When I was in the expo I saw the judges coming to my table and I felt nervous and scared that I was going to stutter even after all the practicing. I first showed my exisiting technologies I researched and I talked about what all of them had in common, and I flowed into my future technology I came up with. I showed the AI generated picture of the teleportation device first, then I showed my A-frame, and explained why I made AI generated model to better visualize the future technology. I finished the pitch with a thank you. Every time I presented I found myself going with what feels right and not word for word of what I had, I was adding ideas as I was currently presenting to the judges or students.
